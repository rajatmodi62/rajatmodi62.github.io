A lot of people in the west want to go to the east. They think that the way of life in east is far simpler. One gets the time to pause, and reflect on what his life means

A lot of people in the east want to go the west. They think that their way of life is too boring. They desire adventure, and crave more independence. 

West offers materialism. East offers spirituality. Both are required. So again the cross pollination occurs. 

It is funny to realize that both east and west have started to invert: In places like India, people are now becoming more independent, and mimicking US culture. In the US, people are now realizing the importance of family. 

The circle of life thus seems to be complete. There is no start or end. There is no source. There is no destination. Time flows in circle. Everything is in recirculation. There is a bottom-up input. There is a top-down feedback. Both are symmetric. 

Universe started from big bang and expanded outwards. At some point in the future, it will become a blackhole. The blackhole shall suck everything around it and once again dissolve into nothing. The circle of life shall then begin again.
